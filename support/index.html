Support

CAMVO FOR iPHONE · iOS 18 OR LATER

Questions, problems or ideas: email iamrkasula@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in Camvo’s Settings. Every message is read.

Common questions

Which looks are there, and do any cost extra?

Four, all included: VHS ’89 (1980s camcorder tape), Super 8 (home film at 18 frames a second), Hi8 ’95 (mid-90s Hi8 camcorder) and Tape ’04 (2000s digital tape). Swipe the viewfinder or tap a label to switch; tap the selected label to open its dials.

Can I change the look after I’ve filmed?

Yes. Open the clip in Clips and tap Change look. This works for clips filmed while Keep clean copies is on in Settings (it is on unless you turn it off). Clean copies use extra space; Settings shows how much and can remove them, after which those clips keep the look they have.

The date stamp shows the wrong date.

Open the look’s dials and tap Date stamp to show today, any date you choose, or no date. The date is drawn only in the picture; the saved file keeps the real date and time you filmed.

I don’t hear the tape sound or projector hum while filming.

They are added to the clip, so you hear them when you play or share it, not in the viewfinder. Choose Original in the dials to keep only the real sound.

Can I use a video or photo from my library?

Yes. In Clips, tap + and pick a video or photo. Camvo only receives what you pick. Choose the look and tap Make it; you can keep filming or close Camvo while it works, and it carries on next time.

What do the “Save and share as” options mean?
  • Best quality: the clip as filmed, 1080p HEVC.
  • Most compatible: 1080p H.264, for older computers and apps.
  • Smaller file: 720p, for messages.

Photos are saved as they are.

How do I join clips into one?

In Clips, tap the select button, pick two or more videos (photos can’t be joined) and tap Join. They play oldest first with a moment of tape snow between them.

The camera says “Paused to save battery”.

After two minutes without use, Camvo pauses the viewfinder to save battery and heat. Tap anywhere, or the record button, to go on. It never pauses while you record or while the dials are open.

My sideways video came out upright.

Camvo follows iPhone’s rotation like the Camera app. With Rotation Lock on, iPhone reports upright, so the clip is upright too. Turn Rotation Lock off in Control Center to film sideways.

Recording stopped by itself.

Camvo stops and saves the clip when a call comes in, when it’s closed, when storage is nearly full or when the iPhone gets too warm. Nothing filmed up to then is lost. If the iPhone is warm, the viewfinder runs at 24 frames a second until it cools.

Can I turn off the vibrations?

Yes: Settings → Haptics.

Does Camvo need an account or the internet?

No account. Filming, looks, saving and sharing work offline. See the privacy policy for the anonymous crash reports and usage counts it sends when online.

Reporting a problem

The quickest way is Send feedback in Settings: it fills in the Camvo version, iPhone model and iOS version for you. Please also tell us:

  • what you did, step by step, and what you expected;
  • which look and dials were on, and whether it was a video or a photo;
  • if you can, a screenshot or a short screen recording.
