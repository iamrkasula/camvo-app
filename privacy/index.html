Privacy Policy

UPDATED 14 NOV 2026

Camvo is a camera that gives your videos and photos the look of old camcorders and home film. The looks are made on your iPhone. What you film never reaches us. The only things that come to us are anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage counts, described below.

What you film

  • Camera. Camvo uses the camera to show the look live in the viewfinder and to record. Every frame is processed on your iPhone.
  • Microphone. Camvo asks for the microphone the first time you record, to record sound with your clips. If you say no, clips are silent.
  • Your clips are stored inside Camvo on your iPhone. With Keep clean copies on (Settings), Camvo also keeps an unprocessed copy of each clip so you can change its look later. You can turn this off and remove the copies in Settings, or delete any clip in Clips. Deleting Camvo removes everything it stored.
  • Photos. To save a clip to Photos, Camvo asks for add-only access: it can add to your library but can’t see what’s in it. Videos and photos you bring in come through Apple’s photo picker, so Camvo only receives the items you pick.
  • Sharing happens only when you tap Share, and goes only where you send it.

Nothing you film leaves your iPhone unless you save or share it. Camvo has no server of its own.

Services Camvo uses

Camvo uses two services. Neither ever receives your videos, photos, sound, clip names, location or anything you type, and neither is used for advertising or tracking.

  • Sentry (crash reports): when the App Store or TestFlight version crashes or freezes, it receives the app and iOS version, iPhone model, technical details of the crash and an anonymous install ID, and for some sessions how long things took. Used only to find and fix problems. Sentry is set never to store your IP address. Privacy policy · Terms
  • TelemetryDeck (usage counts): receives anonymous counts of which features are used (for example, that a clip was saved and with which look), with the app and iOS version, iPhone model, language and an anonymous ID that TelemetryDeck hashes before storing. Used only to see which features matter. Privacy policy · Terms

Both services handle this data under their own privacy policies (linked above), which protect it at least as well as this policy. None of it is linked to your identity or sold.

Keeping and deleting data

Your clips stay on your iPhone until you delete them; deleting Camvo deletes them all. Crash reports and usage counts are kept only as long as the services need them for these purposes, under their own retention rules. To stop sending them, delete Camvo. Because they carry no name, email or device identifier, they can’t be traced to you; if you have a question or a deletion request, email us at the address below.

Feedback email

Send feedback in Settings opens your own Mail app with a new email. Below your message it adds the Camvo version, iPhone model and iOS version, which you can see and delete before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

What Camvo does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location access. Date stamps come from the date you choose, not from where you are.
  • No server of our own: your clips are never sent to us.

Children

Camvo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
